# 30-plan — Plans and Strategies

**Group purpose:** Execution plans, strategies, roadmaps, and sequence reglaments.
Ordered by date; most recent plan supersedes older ones (but old ones kept).

## What lives here
- `YYYY-MM-DD-STRATEGY-topic.md` — strategic direction documents
- `YYYY-MM-DD-REGLAMENT-topic.md` — step-by-step execution sequences
- `YYYY-MM-DD-PLAN-topic.md` — work plans

## Ordering rule
DATE prefix: `YYYY-MM-DD-`. Most recent is the active plan.
Superseded plans: add `Status: superseded` header, keep the file.

## Process
- Plans are created at the start of a phase or after a major synthesis.
- They reference syntheses in 10-synthesis and canon in 00-canon.
- An active plan = owner has reviewed and said "proceed".
