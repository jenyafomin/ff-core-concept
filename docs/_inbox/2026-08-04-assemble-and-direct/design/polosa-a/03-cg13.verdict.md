## 2. Substring-match risk — real, but not a false positive today
Grepped `task-pool.md` directly for `CG-0[1-9]|CG-1[0-4]`: the only literal hits are the range notation
`CG-01..CG-14` (line 48, a summary sentence — matches the "CG-01" token by coincidence, not by intent) and
`CG-11/CG-12/CG-13` inside `T-plan-honesty` (line 41, elaborated §166-175). **CG-02, CG-03, CG-05, CG-06,
CG-07 do not appear anywhere in the file** — I3's "would fail now on exactly these five" claim is confirmed
on real data, not asserted. The self-flagged risk (E2: future CG-ids may not get literal-id treatment, so
substring-match is a convention, not a guarantee) is legitimate and correctly classified as non-blocking for
v1 — but it is currently only a **risk footnote**, not a mandatory acceptance-criterion line in §C's patch
text. Should be promoted into the patch (see §4).

## 3. Circularity (§E4) — contradicts the finding's own §B/§F, not a real open risk
§E4 frames CG-13-covering-itself as unresolved: *"нужно решить на харвесте, считается ли CG-13
поглощённой... иначе скрипт при первом запуске упадёт и на себе."* But the finding's own §B already
establishes, and §F's self-check hook explicitly *expects*, that CG-13 is **already** poglощена right now
via criterion (b) — `T-plan-honesty` (`task-pool.md:41`) literally contains the substring "CG-13 гейт-прозой".
§F states plainly: *"Если падает на CG-11/12/13/14 тоже (они поглощены T-plan-honesty)... баг в критерии
(b)."* So on the very first run, CG-13 does **not** fail on itself — this is empirically settled by the
finding's own evidence, not an open harvest decision. §E4 as currently worded is an internal contradiction:
it presents as an unresolved risk something the document itself proves is already closed. Philosophically the
"gate is a row in the table it audits" self-reference is worth one sentence of acknowledgment, but framing it
as "needs a harvest decision, might fail on itself" is wrong and must be corrected before this lands in the
plan — a future reader who only skims §E will think there's an open blocking question that doesn't exist.

## 4. CI-hook gap (§E3) — correctly named, but not R-b-compliant itself
§E3 is honest that this is diagnostic-only (no pre-commit/CI wiring), and says explicitly not to overclaim
KILL-SHOT-2 closure. Good instinct — but per R-b's own rule (quoted in §A), **an empty/未closed facet must be
either closed or explicitly deferred with a task + home**, not just named in a critic-risk prose paragraph
that may not survive harvest. Right now "wire this into CI/pre-commit" is exactly such a facet: real, named,
but not seeded as a task anywhere in `task-pool.md`. The patch in §C should not ship without either (a) a
follow-up task line (owner TBD, home D0/DV, explicit "not before X") for CI-wiring, or (b) an explicit
owner-accepted deferral with date, per R-b's own three closure conditions. Leaving it as risk-only prose is
the same failure mode this whole finding exists to fix, applied to itself.

## 5. Not designing under CG-12 — correctly handled
§B explicitly declines to design under the root-pult schema, §C's patch repeats the restriction
("НЕ проектировать под root-пульт CG-12"), and §D names the shared-home risk with `doc-tree.mjs`, matching
`00-coorch-framing.md`'s warning and deferring the merge shape to harvest without silently building a second
generator. Consistent with `02-cg12.verdict.md` §4's finding on the same shared-home question. No violation.
